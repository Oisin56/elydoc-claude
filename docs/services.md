## Service Descriptions 

### 1. Online GP Consultations

Suitable for many common non-emergency primary care concerns. This includes medication queries, selected prescriptions, sick certification where appropriate, private referral discussion and follow-up advice for suitable issues.

Where physical examination, urgent care or investigations are required, the doctor will signpost the patient to appropriate in-person services.

This service is not suitable for:

- Medical emergencies
- Chest pain or stroke symptoms
- Severe acute illness
- Conditions requiring regular in-person examination or blood tests

### 2. Weight Management Clinic

GP-led, evidence-based weight management support delivered by specialist-trained GPs. Includes lifestyle advice, metabolic health focus and GLP-1 medication where clinically appropriate.

GLP-1 medication is never guaranteed — suitability is assessed individually. The emphasis is on safety, clinical appropriateness and follow-up. This is sustainable medically supervised care, not a weight loss shortcut.

This service is not suitable for:

- Patients seeking guaranteed medication prescriptions
- Patients unwilling to engage with lifestyle assessment
- Emergency or acute care needs

### 3. Hair Loss Clinic

Discreet, evidence-based assessment and management of male pattern hair loss. Includes initial assessment, discussion of evidence-based treatment options where appropriate and ongoing management for suitable patients.

No guaranteed regrowth claims are made. Treatment outcomes vary between individuals.

This service is not suitable for:

- Hair loss caused by acute illness or medication (requires in-person assessment)
- Patients seeking guaranteed treatment outcomes

### 4. Sick Certification

ElyGP may provide employer and work absence notes where clinically appropriate, following a brief GP consultation.

This service provides employer notes only.

Important limitations:

- ElyGP does NOT currently provide Illness Benefit or IB1 social welfare certification
- Certification is not automatic or guaranteed — clinical assessment is required
- Never use language implying instant or guaranteed certification

### 5. Referrals & Prescriptions

Referrals:

Open private referrals where clinically appropriate. The referral is issued to the patient to forward to the private provider of their choice. Selected imaging referrals may also be provided for suitable MSK injuries.

Chronic lower back pain generally requires in-person assessment before imaging referral and we therefore we do not refer for spinal imaging. 

We do not refer for imaging of organs such as brain/abdomen/renal tract/pelvis/lungs

Prescriptions:

A limited prescription service is available. This may include bridging prescriptions (a short term prescription for a medication you are already appropriately established on), short-term treatments and longer-term treatment for selected suitable conditions.

ElyGP does NOT prescribe:

- Morphine/Oxynorm/Oxycontin/Palexia or other strong opioids
- Benzodiazepines
- Sleeping tablets
- Controlled or restricted drugs
- High tech medications
- Vaccines

## What ElyGP Does Not Manage

The following must be clearly communicated and framed as clinical responsibility, not limitation:

- Medical emergencies of any kind
- Chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe shortness of breath
- Children under 12 years old
- Major trauma
- Suicidal ideation or acute mental health crisis
- Severe acute symptoms
- Complex conditions requiring regular physical examination and blood tests
- ADHD assessment or management or referral to private ADHD clinics
- Pregancy related medical concerns 

Always frame this section positively — ElyGP takes clinical boundaries seriously because patient safety is the priority, not because of service limitations.
